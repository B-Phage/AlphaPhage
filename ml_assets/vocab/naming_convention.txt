vocab_<domain>_v<MAJOR>.<MINOR>.<PATCH>.yaml

Examples:
    vocab_phage_tokens_v1.0.0.yaml
    vocab_host_tokens_v2.1.0.yaml
    vocab_joint_v0.3.2.yaml
    vocab_kmer_6mer_v1.4.0.yaml

Why semantic versioning?
    Because vocab breaking changes matter.

Change	                                                    | Semantic bump
____________________________________________________________|_______________
Add a new token that breaks downstream compatibility	    |  MAJOR
Add new tokens but backward compatible	                    |  MINOR
Fix ordering, metadata, or formatting with no model effect	|  PATCH
